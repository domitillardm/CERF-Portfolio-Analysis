# Final-calibration addendum v0.2-F1 — provisional application conventions

Reference: unchanged Inception-aligned codebook v0.2 and its transcribed Appendix B. Session FULL-20261023-01. The owner authorises calibration documentation followed by full-portfolio A/B/C/D1/D2 scoring; this supersedes the prior pilot-only execution limit, not human review or confidentiality requirements. Original pilot results, original workbooks and codebook v0.2 remain unchanged. This addendum does not claim a human-approved adjudication of the pilot.

## Source rule preserved

Select the highest achieved result level supported by the text: 1 activity described; 2 distinct product/service/completed deliverable; 3 another actor's use or documented next step; 4 external change in policy, behaviour, practice, capacity, cooperation, finance or market activity; 6 information too vague for 1–4. Future potential is a separate Yes/No text-presence field. Result codes classify reported achievements, not independently verified effects. None of the conventions below imposes attachment, numerical baseline, institutional reform, legal bindingness, implementation or independent-verification prerequisites absent from Appendix B.

## Calibrated distinctions

1. **Policy development and decisions.** A provider's delivered draft/recommendations is 2. Another actor using recommendations or requesting a next stage is 3. A described external actor's changed policy position, actual policy-document publication/adoption, financing choice or changed operating approach can be 4, without requiring enacted law or subsequent implementation. The reported change must concern the external actor, not merely the consultant producing another draft. A public actor's completed model-choice decision can itself describe changed behaviour/practice, even if the subsequent tool upgrade is future. Record status exactly (drafted, submitted, published, adopted, implemented), not as interchangeable. If publication of a new policy is clearly asserted, use that reported claim with a publication/adoption uncertainty note rather than demanding inspection as a scoring gate. A direct unresolved contradiction over whether any such change occurred warrants an unresolved code/status; do not force 3 or 4.

2. **Capacity.** A reported gain in an identified actor's knowledge, skills, ability or capability may support 4; no requirement of a later law, resource allocation or changed routine is added. Attendance, positive reception or intended capacity building alone is not a reported gain. A vague general slogan can lack enough detail to support that higher level; retain a supported lower achievement or leave the material ambiguity unresolved. No minimum effect size or quantified test is invented.

3. **Cooperation.** A reported new/changed partnership, working relationship, active collaboration or joint arrangement between identifiable actors can support 4. A non-binding agreement is not automatically capped at 3. An invitation, exchange, interest or proposed next meeting supports 3 only when another actor actually took a documented step; it does not automatically establish changed cooperation. Read the reported effect and status. Recurrent joint work or an actor taking a stated leadership role can describe cooperation change without formal institutionalisation. Label high-ambiguity next-step/change readings for human review, rather than adding structural hurdles.

4. **Another/external actor.** Use the functional role described in monitoring. A separately identifiable ministry, company, institution or beneficiary can satisfy the actor test; a real name is unnecessary. A consultant's or CERF team's own internal continuation is not automatically another actor's use. An organisation mentioned in an internal/private discussion may be another actor; if its role cannot be distinguished and the code depends on that distinction, leave the result unresolved with the supported lower bound and alternatives recorded. No organisational independence or counterfactual causality test is imposed.

5. **Activity, output and plans.** A retrospective action description supports 1 even without a higher result. A described delivered workshop/training/facilitation service supports 2 without a commissioning-document gate. A title/gerund can describe an action but may instead name a plan: inspect all monitoring fields for completion/implementation. A detailed future-only or cancelled-before-start record is not automatically 1 or 6. Record result blank with status 'Unresolved: planned-only/no achieved-result convention' and future Yes when an intention is described. Code 6 is reserved for information too vague for any 1–4. Missing information about a higher achievement alone does not make a clear lower achievement 6. Ambiguity is not a compulsory code-6 bucket.

6. **Later evidence and focal attribution.** A uses only its own original monitoring fields, including retrospective information already contained there. B uses its exact provisional country-track history, including subsequent records. Evidence may link to a focal activity through a project identifier, named output or an unambiguous narrative reference identifying the event/product; a formal activity-ID citation is not mandatory. Unique narrative coreference is sufficient if the two passages are recorded and its basis explained. Topic similarity, the same country, a similar date or an unidentified 'past activities' phrase is not enough. A later result belongs to the earlier focal activity only to the extent that the text links that contribution; do not imply sole causality or assign an entire track endpoint to every member. If several matching earlier events are plausible, retain the existing supported level or unresolved interpretation. A/B changes and retained non-transfer cases must be explained. Identical/overlapping source rows remain separate IDs pending human decisions.

7. **Time scope.** The evidence cut-off is the frozen corrected workbook, not the activity's own date or an invented evaluation cut-off. Preserve original date strings and Stage 1 bounds; overlapping/month-only/yearless dates do not establish precise order. Unknown dates are displayed last, not claimed to occur last. Future statements are classified as plans/expected effects even if a later row reports their achievement; later achievement can inform B where a focal link exists, while the historical future wording remains identifiable.

8. **Highest supported level versus unresolved interpretation.** Do not add formal/stronger evidence gates when a source-level reported change is intelligible. Where the text supports a defensible highest reported level, propose it and flag limitations. Where equally plausible readings are materially incompatible and cannot be resolved from monitoring, retain a null proposed code, a supported lower bound where available, explicit alternatives and a human question. Null is not code 6 and is not an approved final score. All proposals/review issues Pending. Existing scores are not answers.

## Country-track scope and maxima

Keep all exact Stage 1 country/track tuples and stable IDs; do not normalise/merge spelling/case variants or accept proposed assignments. For the clearly labelled exact provisional groups, B/C/D outputs refer to that exact subset and may be incomplete canonical track histories. Preserve missing/placeholder track cases in the existing review groups: A remains possible from each record, but B/C/D are 'Pending membership' without a forced track result. A review group is not a newly accepted CERF track. Other material possible multi-track labels remain flagged and exact-label outputs provisional; no duplicate allocation invented.

C is a monitoring-based synthesis of the agreed exact group evidence, applying the same seven-domain highest-reported-achievement test, with scope/coverage and contrary evidence explicit. It does not require several successful activities, widespread effects, a fully documented causal chain or legal/structural change. It is not generated by copying a numerical maximum of A/B proposals. If membership or material interpretation blocks a defensible track judgement, retain a pending result/status rather than fabricate a level.

D1/D2 are separately computed from new eligible A/B proposals, not CERF-M001's maximum human-checked score. Only codes 1–4 enter ordinal maximum; code 6 is never higher than 4. All eligible information codes 6 with no unresolved records yields Insufficient information. Any null/unresolved eligible records means a calculated numeric maximum is only a **known-score lower bound**, not a settled maximum; mark the D result pending, expose the lower bound and unresolved IDs. No eligible record is silently dropped. All ties retained. Review-group membership blocks track maxima. Preserve planned-only/cancelled statuses and show inclusion/eligibility uncertainty rather than changing the activity universe.

## Evidence, potential, blinding and review

Evidence basis/strength and result code are distinct. All source claims remain monitoring or uninspected-source reports unless actual corroboration is inspected; no causal or independent-outcome validation is asserted. Weak evidence does not automatically reduce an otherwise supported result category. Preserve proposed strength definitions from v0.2 as provisional, not Inception-prescribed weights.

Future Yes means the text describes an intention, plan or expected effect; No means none is stated in readable evidence, not no real-world potential. Contradictory/withdrawn plans can leave the binary proposal pending, with a separate uncertainty field; no third future classification is invented. For C, Yes means at least one stated prospect in group evidence, with heterogeneity described. Do not rank potential as a result.

Prepare monitoring-only projections excluding all prior Human/Codex result/evidence/potential labels and AI rationales. Keep A proposals separate and freeze before contextual B review. Prior chat/pilot exposure cannot be erased: this is not a fully blinded independent-rater experiment. Preserve pilot values and report any calibrated differences in a new comparison, not by editing the pilot. Machine checks establish accounting/provenance/arithmetic/file readability, not inter-rater reliability. No case-study comparison, Git data upload, commit or push is authorised.
