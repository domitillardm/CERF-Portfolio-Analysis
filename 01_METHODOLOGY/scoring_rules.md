# Current scoring rules

Updated 4 November 2026. Working version: **CERF-FULL-20261104-W2**. The owner approved the revised calibration for provisional full application, not final human validation of scores or every methodological extension.

## Authority and scope

1. **Source-authorised classification:** [codebook v0.2, section 2](SCORING_CODEBOOK_DRAFT_v0.2.md#2-source-table--faithful-transcription), a transcription of the supplied Inception Report Appendix B excerpt. The full report citation and surrounding methodology remain unavailable.
2. **Agreed working application:** compatible [F1 conventions](CALIBRATION_ADDENDUM_v0.2-F1.md), the revised calibration and subsequent owner instructions recorded in [the decision register](../06_DECISIONS/decision_register.csv). These set the current focal-only scope, evidence vocabulary and maximum calculation.
3. **Not adopted:** historical contextualised activity, holistic synthesis and A/B/C/D1/D2 workflows; proposed evidence-strength scales; unresolved case judgements. Their presence in unchanged source documents is not current authorisation.

## Classification definitions

| Code | Exact classification | Appendix B test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Activity described | Does the text only explain what CERF did? |
| 2 | Output delivered | Does the text confirm a distinct product, service or completed deliverable? |
| 3 | Concrete use or follow-up reported | Did another actor use the output or take a documented next step? |
| 4 | Change in an external actor reported | Does the text describe a change in policy, behaviour, practice, capacity, cooperation, finance or market activity? |
| 6 | Insufficient information | Is the information too vague to support codes 1–4? |

Assign the highest directly supported **achieved** result using only the focal record. Code 6 is an information status, not an achievement level above 4 or proof of failure. Lack of evidence for 4 does not justify 6 if a lower achieved level is clear. Leave a materially unresolved highest classification blank, recording alternatives, uncertainty and a supported lower bound where defensible; do not force a result to increase coverage.

## Evidence and potential

Use the original Activity Universe variable meanings and approved worksheet adaptations. Recover only explicit same-record statements from alternative fields, retaining qualifications, original source references and ORANGE provenance. GREEN is direct extraction. Neither route measures strength. Titles, goals and selected indicators alone do not establish delivery, use or change. Read all relevant focal text, including comments, without importing other activities' results.

Evidence Basis uses four **provisional controlled categories**: **Monitoring claim only; Named or linked source; Independently corroborated; Unclear or missing**. A named source is not inspected corroboration; repetition is not independence. No extra requirement for independent verification, quantified change, legal bindingness or institutional reform is added to the source tests.

Future Potential remains a separate **Yes/No** field: Yes when an intention, plan or expected effect is stated alongside or instead of achievement; No means not stated in readable text, not no real-world potential. Pending is recorded as uncertainty, not a new classification. Planned-only, actor-role, conflicting-claim and activity-boundary cases remain visible for human review where the record cannot settle them.

Every proposal must preserve exact excerpts, Activity Universe variables, original workbook/worksheet/row/cells or comments, Evidence Basis, justification, Future Potential, uncertainty, review state, identifiers and dataset/rule versions. Record human decisions separately. Track uncertainty alone does not prevent a supported focal score.

## Country-track maximum

For each accepted country-track, calculate the maximum supported eligible activity achievement among **1–4 only**. Code 6 must never outrank 4. All eligible code-6 records with no unresolved members mean insufficient information, without a numeric achievement maximum. Keep unresolved membership, eligibility and scores explicit; incomplete groups stay pending, even when a known lower bound is 4. Show known-score lower bounds, supporting activities and all ties. Do not create an Unknown track or silently drop null members. No eligible members means an explicit no-eligible-result status, not 0.

Current outputs are provisional monitoring classifications, not independently verified outcomes or causal findings. The separate final evidence-standardisation one-pager is still unavailable; the approved matrix and explicit owner rules are the working standard, with future document concordance pending.
