# CERF portfolio methodology — client appendix draft

**Not approved for client circulation.** This is an internal summary scaffold; designated human review and release approval are required.

The current working portfolio comprises 182 reconstructed monitoring records. Activity proposals classify the highest directly supported achieved result from the focal record: activity described (1), output delivered (2), concrete use or follow-up reported (3), external actor change reported (4). Insufficient information (6) is not an achievement level. Future Potential and Evidence Basis remain separate. Explicit alternative-field recoveries retain source provenance and qualifications.

Country-track maxima use eligible accepted-member achievement codes 1–4; incomplete groups remain pending with known-score lower bounds and supporting activities. No current contextualised or holistic track score is produced. Monitoring-based AI proposals have passed technical checks but still require human validation; they do not establish independent outcomes or CERF causality.

Before completing this appendix, obtain reviewed classifications, confirmed group scope, current denominators, full source citation and the final standardisation document.
